Troubleshooting guide

Internet not working? Try these quick fixes before reporting an issue.

Step 1

Restart your router

Unplug the power adapter, wait 30 seconds, then plug it back in. Give it 2–3 minutes to fully start before testing again.

Step 2

Check the lights & cables

Make sure the power and fiber cables are firmly plugged in and not bent or pinched. A red or blinking LOS/alarm light usually means a fiber problem — don’t touch the fiber cable, report it instead.

Step 3

Reconnect to your WiFi

On your phone or laptop, turn WiFi off and on, or “forget” the network and connect again with your WiFi password. Move closer to the router if the signal is weak.

Step 4

Try another device

Check if the problem is on one device only or on all of them. If just one device is affected, restart that device. If everything is offline, the problem is with the connection.

Step 5

Check your account

Look at your Amount To Pay and due date above. An overdue bill can pause your service — once your payment is settled, your connection should return.

Still no luck?

Report an issue

If nothing above worked, send us a report and a technician will be assigned. You can follow the progress right here on your home screen.
